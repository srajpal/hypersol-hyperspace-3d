**Title:** HyperSpace 3D: a web browser with a Three.js interface, and
HoloML, 3D web pages drawn with Three.js

**Post:**

**[owner: upload docs/video/hyperspace-3d.gif here, with the alt text
"Harbour Loft in HyperSpace 3D"]**
>
> Two open-source projects (Apache 2.0) built on Three.js:
>
> **HyperSpace 3D** is an experimental desktop browser (Electron). Its
> interface is a Three.js room: the live web page sits on a tilted
> panel, placed with CSS 3D transforms so it stays sharp and takes
> clicks, tabs are cards in an arc beside it, and a page's pictures
> and glTF models can be lifted off it into the room, drawn by a second
> WebGL canvas with the room's camera.
>
> **HoloML** is a small markup language for 3D pages, and its viewer
> is Three.js: glTF 2.0 with Draco, meshopt, and KTX2; shadows; light
> from a panorama; a sky; water with haze and moving light added to
> every material in onBeforeCompile; shaders compiled without holding
> up the page; groups of models loaded only while you are near, with
> stand-ins; lighter models far away; and no frames drawn while
> nothing moves.
>
> The examples open in any browser with WebGL 2:
>
> - Harbour Loft, a flat to walk through: https://srajpal.github.io/hypersol-hyperspace-3d/viewer/?page=https://srajpal.github.io/holoml/harbour-loft/index.holoml
> - The ocean tunnel, an aquarium: https://srajpal.github.io/hypersol-hyperspace-3d/viewer/?page=https://srajpal.github.io/holoml/aquarium/index.holoml
> - Blockworld, a small block game: https://srajpal.github.io/hypersol-hyperspace-3d/viewer/?page=https://srajpal.github.io/holoml/blockworld/index.holoml
> - All seven: https://srajpal.github.io/hypersol-hyperspace-3d/
>
> Most models are from Poly Haven and Kenney (CC0); the sneaker store's
> shoe and most of the ocean tunnel's fish are CC BY 4.0, credited in
> HoloML's NOTICE and each example's models/CREDITS.md.
>
> Code: https://github.com/srajpal/hypersol-hyperspace-3d (the viewer
> is apps/browser/src/viewer, the room apps/browser/src/renderer/scene)
> and https://github.com/srajpal/holoml. Questions about how any of it
> is drawn are very welcome.
