HyperSol HyperSpace 3D

An experimental open-source desktop web browser with a 3D interface, and the home of HoloML pages in the browser. The project lives at github.com/srajpal/hypersol-hyperspace-3d; this page will grow into its landing page.

The HoloML viewer for ordinary browsers shows a HoloML page on a plain web page: viewer/?page= followed by the page's address. The HoloML examples open in it with one click.
