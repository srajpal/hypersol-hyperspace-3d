# Code of conduct

HyperSpace 3D is a personal open-source project. Everyone who takes
part in it (in issues, pull requests, reviews, and anywhere else the
project is discussed) is asked to keep to this.

## What is expected

- Be respectful. Talk about the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and say plainly what you mean: what you saw, what
  you expected, what you suggest.
- Accept that others may decide differently; the maintainer has the
  last word on what is merged.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or attacks on anyone, for any reason.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else that would make a reasonable person feel unwelcome.

## If something goes wrong

Tell the maintainer privately. Until a contact address is published,
use GitHub's private reporting on this repository (Security, then
Report a vulnerability) and say that the report is about conduct; it
reaches only the maintainer. Reports are kept in confidence.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull
requests, and block accounts that do not keep to this.
